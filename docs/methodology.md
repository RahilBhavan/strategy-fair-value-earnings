# Earnings bridge, eligibility screen and conditional scenarios

Financial cutoff: 2026-10-21; last complete price candle: 2026-10-20 UTC. All results are USD unless labeled otherwise. Observation dates differ from fetch timestamps, which may be October 22 UTC while still October 21 in Detroit.

The reported bridge uses fair-value-era quarters Q1 2025 through Q2 2026. With holdings H, price P and original cost C, the no-sales gain bridge is `H_end*P_end - H_begin*P_begin - (C_end-C_begin)`. When sales occur, add proceeds minus original cost removed. The June 2026 filing supplies $83.205 million proceeds and $168.126 million cost removed. Including that correction reconciles the filing-implied-price gain bridge for all six reported quarters. This is an accounting reconciliation using end-period filed inputs, not a prospective trading signal. No-sales is still an explicit assumption in future simulations.

A blended tax coefficient is calibrated from the January 2025 adoption balance sheet. The tax proxy changes with unrealized gains above cost and assumes a full deferred-tax-asset valuation allowance below cost. Other pretax income is the median of the previous four available quarters. The same proxy is applied across the sample despite changes in actual tax treatment. Consequently the net-income backtest has material residuals even when the asset-gain bridge reconciles. The Coinbase-price backtest additionally uses UTC closes rather than the company's 4 p.m. ET measurement.

The latest reported screen uses June 30, 2026: latest net income -$8,219,628,000 and trailing four -$30,413,926,000. Both fail positivity. Reported net income is used as the continuing-operations measure for this issuer; there is no generic discontinued-operations adjustment engine. The September quarter is separate: a preliminary company asset-gain/deferred-tax estimate plus modelled other income, not reported GAAP net income. Its net-income estimate and any screen incorporating it are explicitly modelled.

The [July 2026 S&P U.S. Indices Methodology](https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/documents/methodologies/methodology-sp-us-indices.pdf), financial viability section, requires positive continuing-operations GAAP earnings for the latest quarter and sum of four consecutive quarters. Passing that condition does not imply selection. The model does not assess the other criteria or committee discretion. Quarterly checkpoint dates and filing lags are assumed evaluation dates, not promised addition dates. Input sequences must be chronological; dated-series evaluation skips missing quarters.

The bootstrap samples blocks of 20 historical daily log returns, seed 20261021, for 20,000 paths per earnings regime. `demeaned` removes historical log drift; `raw` retains it; `recent` uses the last four years with their drift. Future holdings/cost stay at the October 18 8-K snapshot; financing, sales, reserves, preferred dividends and other income do not dynamically react to BTC. An after-preferred sensitivity subtracts the latest known preferred burden. The July 2026 methodology says "GAAP net income from continuing operations" without defining it or mentioning preferred dividends (checked 2026-10-22), so the basis is unsettled; the headline uses the 10-Q's "Net (loss) income" line, before "Dividends on preferred stock". These probabilities are conditional Monte Carlo frequencies, not calibrated market odds, real outcomes, or S&P selection probabilities. Q3 inputs remain preliminary even when an output labels a checkpoint deterministic: that label means no simulated future quarter is needed. Each checkpoint row carries a `reason`; the December 18, 2026 frequency is 0 because the four quarters ending September 30, 2026 sum to a loss even with the modelled Q3 gain.
